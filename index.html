Research Workflow

  1. Collect public vulnerability intelligence.
  2. Check exact program scope and policy.
  3. Verify affected versions and potential impact.
  4. Review reproducible evidence and duplicates.
  5. Manually approve any report before submission.

Safety Rules

Only authorized, in-scope research. No destructive testing, data theft, credential attacks, persistence, denial of service or unauthorized access. Public CVEs are leads, not proof of a bounty-eligible finding.
